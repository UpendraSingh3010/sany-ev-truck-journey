# SANY EV Funnel Brand Realignment

## Goal
Rework the current funnel so it feels like a focused SANY India campaign page, using the original website as the visual authority while preserving the supplied EV-truck story and enquiry flow.

## Brand direction
- Replace the current dark cinematic treatment with SANY India’s brighter corporate system: deep navy header, white and pale-grey content areas, SANY red branding and primary actions, and cyan only as a supporting product colour.
- Use Roboto throughout with practical corporate sizing and hierarchy rather than oversized condensed display text.
- Present the SANY wordmark in its familiar red brand block and make “Get a Quote” the clear red conversion action.
- Remove the neon-tech feel, including scanning lines, glowing effects, orbiting labels, oversized technical codes, and overly dark image treatments.

## Layout changes
1. Build a SANY-style two-level header with brand/contact treatment above a clear section navigation row.
2. Recompose the opening as a wide, bright truck-led banner with concise copy, a red quote action, and compact specification highlights.
3. Keep all ten “Without SANY / With SANY” stages, but place them in alternating white and light-grey horizontal sections.
4. Present comparisons as clear editorial columns with restrained red and cyan labels, large supplied photography, and simple factual callouts.
5. Replace decorative technical overlays with clean diagrams, specification rules, and brochure-like annotation.
6. Redesign the final system-convergence section as a structured component-to-truck diagram instead of an orbit animation.
7. Restyle the quote area and fields to match SANY’s square, high-contrast corporate controls, with a prominent red submit button.
8. Use a compact dark corporate footer consistent with the header.

## Content and behavior
- Preserve the supplied claims, truck models, ten-stage order, imagery, and demo-only form confirmation.
- Keep quote navigation, mobile navigation, form validation, and reduced-motion support working.
- Do not invent prices, specifications, contact details, or additional product claims.

## Technical details
- Rework the home page structure and global semantic design tokens around the confirmed SANY reference.
- Keep colour values centralized in the design system and reuse the existing button component.
- Retain the existing page metadata and ensure the page remains accessible and readable without animation.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile widths, including image loading, navigation, quote submission, text fit, and horizontal overflow.
